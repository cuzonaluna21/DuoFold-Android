## 🪄 What Is DuoFold-Android?

DuoFold-Android is a magical app that makes your regular phone behave like an expensive foldable phone. It creates a system-wide "fold illusion" across your entire screen. The app uses your phone's built-in motion sensors to reproject and reshape what you see, giving you that cool dynamic folding effect without needing any special hardware. It works on any standard Android phone, no root required, thanks to the clever use of OpenGL ES and Shizuku.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Whole-Screen Illusion:** The effect applies to your entire interface, not just one app.
- **Real Motion Tracking:** Your device's accelerometer and gyroscope drive the fold effect for a natural, responsive feel.
- **No Special Hardware:** Works perfectly on standard, non-foldable phones.
- **No Root Needed:** Powered by Shizuku, which runs without rooting your device.
- **Smooth Performance:** Built on OpenGL ES for high-quality graphics and seamless animations.
- **System-Wide Integration:** Once activated, the fold effect follows you across all your apps and screens.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Problem:** The fold effect is not showing up.
**Solution:** Make sure the master toggle in the app is ON. Also confirm that Shizuku is running in the background. Open the Shizuku app to check its status and restart the service if it shows "disconnected."

**Problem:** The screen looks too distorted.
**Solution:** Lower the **Bend Factor** slider in the DuoFold-Android app. Even a small value (around 20-30%) provides a noticeable fold effect without being overwhelming.

**Problem:** The app is not responding to my movements.
**Solution:** Check that your phone's auto-rotate feature is enabled in the Quick Settings Menu. Also, gently calibrate your phone by moving it in a figure-8 pattern once after opening the app.

**Problem:** The app closes immediately or shows an error.
**Solution:** Make sure you have granted all the required permissions (e.g., display over other apps, notification access). Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > DuoFold-Android** and check "Permissions." If the issue persists, restart your phone, then try again.

**Problem:** I don't understand how to set up Shizuku.
**Solution:** Shizuku setup sounds complicated, but for most non-rooted users, it means enabling "Wireless debugging" in Android developer options, then following a step-by-step command in a computer terminal once. The DuoFold-Android app provides a clickable, friendly guide directly on its setup screen. Just follow the numbers.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Do I need to root my phone?**
No. DuoFold-Android uses Shizuku, which does not require a root. It works on most Android 8.0 and above devices.

**Will this damage my phone's screen?**
No. It is a software effect. It only changes how the content is rendered visually. It does not physically bend or stress your screen.

**Does it work while watching videos or playing games?**
Yes. Because it is system-wide, the fold illusion applies to every app, including video players and games. However, you may want to lower the effect intensity for games to minimize distraction.

**Is the app free?**
DuoFold-Android is offered as a free application on the provided GitHub link.

**Will this drain my battery faster?**
Yes, slightly. Since the GPU is doing extra rendering work continuously, you might see a small increase in battery consumption. It is recommended to turn off the effect when you do not need it.

**Can I use a regular mouse or keyboard with this?**
The fold effect is designed for touch and motion. While it will display on an external monitor (if your phone supports HDMI-out), the effect is optimized for handheld usage.
